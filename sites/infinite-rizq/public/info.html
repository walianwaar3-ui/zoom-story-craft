Before you come

  • Bring your ticket on your phone. Lost it? Get it at infiniterizq.com/my-seat with your email.
  • Your seat is given to you at check-in. Sisters sit on the left of the centre aisle, brothers on the right.
  • Student pass holders: both students show a valid student card at the door.
  • Paid in the last hours? Bring your payment screenshot.
  • Please arrive by 10:15 AM. Doors close at 10:30 for the opening; latecomers are seated at the first pause.
  • One full day, in person only. There is no recording or replay.
  • Tea and snacks are served for every guest. VIP Inner Table guests have a private buffet inside the hotel.
