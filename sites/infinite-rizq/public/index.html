Who we are

Not another seminar. A room where the work happens.

World Wealth Organization, founded by Shahrez Hayder, works where business meets the soul: how people think, decide, handle fear, and trust Allah while taking action.

Infinite Rizq is that work, live and in person. Guests don’t just listen. They do the work, pen in hand, through the Reveal, Kill, Rewire and Expand processes, built on an Islamic understanding of rizq.
