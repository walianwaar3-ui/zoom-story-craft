Before you decide

Straight answers.

Is this just another seminar?

No. It’s an interactive transformation session. You’ll actively participate in exercises and practical processes (Reveal, Kill, Rewire, Expand) with a pen in hand. You’ll spend more time doing the work than listening.

Will this teach me how to make money?

It isn’t a business course. It works on the beliefs, fears, identity and spiritual condition that shape how you recognise, pursue and manage rizq. That is the layer most business training never touches.

Who should attend? Can women attend?

Anyone, men and women, willing to challenge their beliefs, confront internal limitations and actively participate: students, professionals, founders, and those seeking marriage or more peace. If you only want to listen, this isn’t for you.

Will this guarantee wealth or remove all my blockages?

No. And anyone who promises that is lying to you. Rizq is from Allah Subhanahu wa Ta’ala alone. The session gives you practical processes to identify and begin working through your blockages, and tools to keep applying them. Results depend on the work you continue to do.

Is it Islamically grounded?

Yes. It is built on an Islamic understanding of rizq: Allah Subhanahu wa Ta’ala as Ar-Razzaq, tawakkul alongside responsible action, personal responsibility, gratitude, and wealth as an amanah. Not shortcuts or superstition.

Will there be a recording or online option?

No. There are no recordings, no replays and no playback access. This is a one-time event, and the experience is only available to those physically present.

General Admission is PKR 11,111. The Pair Pass is PKR 15,555 in total for two people. The Inner Table is PKR 33,333 and is limited to 15 seats.
