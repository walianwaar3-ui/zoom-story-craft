Before you decide

Straight answers.

Is this just another motivational seminar?

No. It’s a participatory working session — guided reflection, written exercises and a personal action plan. You’ll spend more time writing than listening.

Will this teach me how to make money?

It isn’t a business course. It works on the beliefs, fears, identity and spiritual perspective that shape how you pursue, decide on and manage wealth — the layer most business training never touches.

Is it only for entrepreneurs? Can women attend?

It’s for anyone serious about growth — students, professionals, founders, and those seeking marriage or more peace. Men and women are both welcome.

Will this guarantee wealth or marriage?

No — and anyone who promises that is lying to you. Rizq is from Allah alone. What you get is clarity on the patterns you control, practical tools to start changing them, and a written plan to act on.

Is it Islamically grounded?

Yes. The framework is built on Allah as Ar-Razzaq, tawakkul alongside responsible action, gratitude, and wealth as an amanah — not on shortcuts or superstition.

Will there be a recording or online option?

No. It’s in person only. The work happens in the room, and it isn’t being recorded or streamed.

What if I can’t attend after booking?

With only 200 seats, tickets are non-refundable. You can transfer your seat to someone else — just contact us before the event and we’ll update the name.
